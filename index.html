Medieval Marionette

✦   an interactive experiment   ✦

Afun interactive experiment written and coded by Victora Craggs as part of an ongoing research and design exploration into medieval reception and movement. All elements can be edited, and by viewing the GitHub Repository (instructions given in the Meet a Medievalist Maker post), you can create and edit your own! Just like the medieval text, coding repositories are palimpsestic sites of writing and rewriting - and, coding should always be for the people, with the people, from the people.
