The Marionette

✦   an interactive curiosity   ✦

Behold two pilgrims of olde — the Knight and his Squire — suspended upon four invisible strings apiece. Tied to thy fingertips by the magick of the looking-glass, they shall walk, sway, and bow at thy command. Permit the glass to see thee, and raise thy hand into view.
